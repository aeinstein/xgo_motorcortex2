Recolor the floor to flat solid #00FF00 and only #00FF00. The floor is the horizontal ground surface. Floor underneath furniture counts only where the gap above it is higher than 18 cm, for example under tables and desks between their legs. Floor under lower things, such as chair bases with wheels, sofas, beds, low shelves and cabinets, stays unchanged. Baseboards and skirting tiles along the bottom of the walls are part of the wall, even if they are made of the same tiles as the floor: they stay unchanged. Shadows, seams and reflections on the floor belong to the floor. Rugs and carpets are not floor: leave every existing rug unchanged. Things lying or standing on the floor, such as boxes, bags, shoes and cables, are not floor: they stay unchanged. A thin transparent chair mat lying flat on the floor is floor. Do not add, remove or change anything else, and do not draw any new object.
