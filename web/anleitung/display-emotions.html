    Item Value
    Eye generator if the daemon reads /tmp/lcd0.augen and lists the name in /tmp/lcd0.augen.namen: one line ausdruck NAME instead of frames (colour from a folder emotions_<colour>), waits until the daemon clears /tmp/lcd0.owner; no reader within 0.5 s or unknown name: raw frames as below
    Frames /opt/xgonav/emotions, generated by tools/eyes.py in deploy/install.sh
    Fallback no .raw files → decodes PNGs with ffmpeg
    CPU (RiderPi) 55 frames ~0.18 s (old ffmpeg path: 1.04 s for 24 frames)
    Ownership newest player writes its PID to /tmp/lcd0.owner; older players exit before next frame (works across users). Also used by the intercom image (tools/sprech.php, web-interface.md)
    /tmp/lcd0.owner created by rc.local: root, group video, 0664 (fs.protected_regular)

    Eye animations (tools/eyes.py)¶

    Stylized eyes only, one color on black (antialiased), same output format as emotion.sh build.

    python tools/eyes.py OUTDIR [--color R,G,B] [--only NAME]... [--png] [--preview DIR]
python tools/eyes.py --list
python tools/eyes.py --gaze DIR      # eye atlas for mode pet: 11 gaze positions (10 steps) x 5 lid positions, 55 frames, 8.4 MB
python tools/eyes.py OUTDIR --farbe rot   # colour from PALETTE (rot, rosa, gelb, gruen, lila); --palette lists them

    Eye colours per person (faces.md): deploy/install.sh renders every PALETTE colour to /opt/xgonav/emotions_<colour>/ and /opt/xgonav/eyes_gaze_<colour>/ (≈ 170 MB each, once; stamp = md5 of eyes.py). While pet recognises a person with an eye colour (and [pet] eye_hold_s = 20 s after), Emotions.farbe plays emotions from the colour folder and lcd.py shows the colour atlas (loaded once, at most one kept); missing folder → turquoise.

    Item Value
    Output OUTDIR/<Name>/0001.raw ..., 320×240 RGB565 LE, 153 600 bytes (same as emotion.sh build)
    Length 55–110 frames at 25 fps (2.2–4.4 s), ~1090 frames / 167 MB in total (per colour)
    Loop first and last frame = neutral eyes (except sleep: ends closed)
    Color default turquoise 0,225,210
    --png also writes <Name><n>.png
    --preview DIR contact sheet <Name>.png and <Name>.mp4 per emotion
    Runtime ~7 s on a PC for all 15; needs only numpy + OpenCV
    Install deploy/install.sh → /opt/xgonav/emotions; the web UI lists this directory
    Emotion Eyes
    Angry lids slanted down toward the center, trembling, two pulses
    angst wide eyes, small pupils darting left/right, worried lids (inner corners up), trembling (fear of the dark)
    aufwachen eyes start closed, open halfway, close again, open fully, look left and right, one blink (boot)
    cute large round eyes without pupils, smiling lower edge, gentle bob, head tilt, short ^ ^ squint
    doubt one eye raised, other squinted, glance right then left
    embarrassed darting glances left/right, then trembling > <
    grievance round pleading eyes looking up, sad lids, quivering lower lid, tear
    hate eye roll upward, then narrowed frowning slits, side glance
    laugh ^ ^ arcs bouncing
    like heart eyes beating
    sad lids drooping outward, eyes sink, slow blink
    shy smaller half-closed eyes looking down/aside, one glance back
    sleep lids get heavy, two jerks awake, closed ‿ ‿, breathing
    surprised eyes pop to large circles (overshoot), double blink
    vertigo rotating spirals circling in opposite phase

    Eye generator in the display daemon¶

    Same model as tools/eyes.py, drawn live by st7789_server (2026-11-28). xgonav, tools/emotion.sh and the web UI send text lines, nothing is pre-rendered for it. On the Rider since 2026-11-28, checked lying (expressions, pet eyes, cost); not seen while driving.

    Item Value
    Source st7789/project/riderpi/src/augen.c (raster), augen_ausdruck.c (the 15 expressions, ported line by line), augen_leben.c (autonomous layer, commands), augen.h (ABI 1)
    Input FIFO /tmp/lcd0.augen (created by the daemon, root:video 0660), one command per line (< 128 bytes, atomic)
    Names /tmp/lcd0.augen.namen: expressions the daemon knows (emotion.sh uses the generator only for these)
    Raster signed distance per pixel inside the eye rectangles, coverage clamp(0.65 − d) (cv2 LINE_AA draws ~0.15 px larger), strokes +0.1 px; sampled at (i + 0.375, j + 0.375) like INTER_AREA of the 4× mask
    Match with tools/eyes.py mean ≤ 0.13 %, area ≤ 2 %, summed ≤ 5.4 % (sleep: distance to the flat half-ellipse approximated)
    Frame rate 25 fps while something moves (expression, saccade, blink, colour fade), 10 fps while only breathing, without breathing only at the next blink or microsaccade (≤ 1 s), none when off
    SPI only the union of old and new eye rectangle (window of st7789_basic_draw_picture_16bits)
    Cost raster per frame: kimmy 0.16–0.4 ms; CM4 at 1.5 GHz 1.2–3.4 ms (mean 2.1 ms, living eyes 1.4 ms), at the idle clock (600–700 MHz, ondemand) ~5 ms. Daemon CPU on the Rider 2026-11-28 (idle clock): off 0 %, living eyes without breathing 1.7 %, with breathing 5.6 % (10 fps), expressions back to back 16 % of a core; pet lying with image processing (1.5 GHz) 4.1 %. /tmp/lcd0.augen.stat every 10 s: frames/s, µs raster, µs SPI, pixels
    Priority raw frames on /tmp/lcd0 win: generator pauses 0.5 s after the last one and while another PID owns /tmp/lcd0.owner (< 10 s old). During an expression the daemon writes its own PID there (xgonav pauses its output, a newer player aborts the expression) and discards raw frames
    Off [emotion] augen_fifo = ""; without a daemon with generator xgonav uses the atlas and emotion.sh raw frames
    Command Effect
    an / aus living eyes on (pet) / off, last frame stays; aus puts gaze, vergence and lid back to the centre (otherwise later expressions kept the last gaze, Rider 2026-11-28)
    ausdruck NAME [STRENGTH] expression once; with an blended over the living eyes (0.15 s in/out, gaze kept), otherwise as pre-rendered (sleep ends closed)
    blick X Y −1..1; X > 0 = right in the image (eye moves 34 px, eye on that side 10 % wider), Y > 0 = down (22 px); change > 0.15 = saccade (30–110 ms, 50 % blink above 0.5), smaller follows (τ 0.12 s)
    nah M distance of the fixated target (m, 0 = off); below 0.6 m the eyes move together (up to 9 px each)
    lid L [S] base lid 0 open .. 1 shut, transition S s
    farbe RRGGBB\|NAME [S] colour, cross-fade S s (default 0.3); names tuerkis, rot, rosa, gelb, gruen, lila
    rate N / blinzeln blinks per minute (0 = none; random, ≥ 1.2 s apart, 10 % double blinks) / one blink now
    atmen 0\|1 breathing ±0.8 px at 0.25 Hz
    abbrechen end the running expression

    Always on: microsaccades every 1–3 s (≤ 1.4 px).

    pet with the generator: PetBrain.augen_ziel = (x, y, lid, distance, rate) per control step, lcd.py sends only changes (≤ 24 lines/s). x stepless from the bearing (no hysteresis); y: standing person with head above the image −0.6, close (< 1.5 m) −0.3, otherwise −0.1, animal on the floor 0.3 (< 1 m) / 0.15; lid 0.5 when fixating (eyes_fix_lid / 4) and when dozing or lying (rate 6/min), 1 in nap/set down (rate 0), lugen opens over eyes_open_s; awake rate 15/min. Person colour (gruss.json augen) as farbe NAME. Leaving pet, Wi-Fi setup, sleep mode → aus.

    Test / tool Content
    tests/test_augen.py needs libxgoaugen.so (XGONAV_AUGENLIB) or gcc, skipped on the Windows PC: parameters of all 15 expressions equal tools/eyes.py, raster vs render(), LUT, autonomous layer (blink rate, saccade, lid, vergence, blending, commands)
    tests/test_augen_zeilen.py lines of xgonav/augen.py, Emotions via the generator
    python tools/augen_vergleich.py DIR contact sheet Python / C / difference per expression
    make -C st7789/project/riderpi augen-lib builds libxgoaugen.so

    Mode pet (xgonav/pet.py)¶

    Started by hand (web UI „Haustier“, --mode pet). Not run on the Rider yet, see limitations.md.

    Trigger Reaction
    person > 3 m notices, chirp
    person 1.5–3 m turns toward, tilts head (roll), doubt; a drive command (v ≠ 0, not turning) sets roll back to 0 and levels once (Robot.request_level, 2026-11-22)
    person < 1.5 m cute, trill, wiggle
    person < ~0.5 m shy, ducks
    box top free and < 1.9 m (kneeling, sitting, child) cute (like only greets known faces, faces.md)
    person approaching > ~1 m/s within 1.6 m surprised, squeak, duck, optional jerk back
    person gone < 3 s and back laugh, giggle
    person gone > 3 s looks after them, doubt, searches (below), sad if not found
    wheels move without a drive command embarrassed, „hey“
    nobody for 30 s (roam_after_s, again every 45 s) roams (below)
    nobody for 60 s / 180 s / 600 s sighs / dozes, detector 1 Hz, roaming ends / sets down, idle
    cat turns toward, like, coo; optional creep up to 0.8 m
    dog within 2 m (priority over all) keeps looking, grievance, whimper, duck, optional jerk back
    dog < 0.5 m or hard scare (with faint) faint, see below

    Eyes (with the eye generator: above, stepless, with vertical gaze, vergence and own blinks; without it the atlas): follow the bearing of the target, gliding one position per control step; once the same person has been fixated for eyes_fix_s (1.5 s, gaze arrived) the lids narrow to slits (eyes_fix_lid 2, 2026-11-22). They blink through all lid positions in 0.4 s (atlas, neutral eye style without pupils, replaces the overlay on the LCD; written only when changed, checked at ~33 Hz). Priority: dog, cat, person. Trust (0..1) shifts thresholds; repeated scares weaken (habituation).

    Search and roam¶

    Tasks inside state wach; any person/cat/dog in view ends them (search: cute + found sound; roam: surprised). Position from wheels and heading (World.yaw), independent of the room map; reset on stand-up.

    Task Sequence
    follow (any person in view whose box is not wider than tall; no trust threshold; 2026-11-22) exactly the routine of mode follow (behaviors.Follow inside Pet): behavior.follow_dist_m 0.9 m, speed, backwards up to backup_v when she comes too close, heights, detector at its full rate; lost → drives to the last place and looks around (map), without map turns to the last side. Ends when follow waits (lost_home: then home), animal in view, she sits/lies, no drive allowed (follow_m, battery, floor); new start after follow_repeat_s. Person lying/sitting (box wider than tall): no following, turns toward her, trust grows, babbles every wide_babble_s 6 s with head tilt. Simulator (--scene zwei) and unit tests only
    search (person gone > gone_s, or lost while following) drive to seek_stop_m (0.4 m) short of the last foot-point position (planner with room map), at most seek_m → look around → sad, lost sound. Sound is ignored while driving there and looking around; afterwards he stays and listens (normal hearing), no drive home (lost_home false, 2026-11-22)
    roam (nobody for roam_after_s) look around, note free space per view → leg of roam_leg_m (0.4 m) toward the freest view within roam_radius_m (1.0 m) of the stand-up spot (none: back toward it) → look around → … until roam_m is used up; new budget after each encounter
    look around look_steps (4) views, look_step_deg (90°) apart, look_pause_s (1.5 s) standing still each (detector, floor model); eyes sweep. Turning only, on by default (look, needs gestures)

    Driving (seek_m, roam_m, roam_radius_m: 0 = off, −1 = no limit; RiderPi default −1 since 2026-11-13, simulator 0 / 0 / 1.0 m) only forward at drive_v (0.12, sim 0.08), with wheels, valid floor model + local map, battery ≥ roam_battery (40 %). With room map and a planned path (since 2026-11-23, before: call search only) the path is driven by PathFollower like home (narrow passages, push, replanning); stops on: remaining distance within coast (~0.9 s × speed), budget, time-out, radius, stuck after two replans. Without path (no room map): straight to the spot; additionally stops on arc check of the local map blocked (path_blocked, computed only while driving), centre free space < half roam_free_min, no progress for 2.5 s. Three failed legs end roaming. Foot points > 5 m (occluded) are ignored, distances capped at 3.5 m. Steps are seen too late by the floor model: never near stairs. Pi cost: arithmetic per control step, plus one arc check per control step while driving.

    Hearing (sound direction, audio.md)¶

    Input: reports of xgonav/ortung.py (≈ 1 per second of sound, direction folded to the front). Direction counts only while balancing and only if the 1 s window began after his own last movement. [pet] hear = false turns all of it off. Unit tests only, not run on the Rider (2026-11-13).

    Situation Reaction
    sound from the side, nobody in view eyes move there, sound horch, turns to it at once (look_w; a sound near the front needs two reports, with hear_seek one), listens hear_listen_s (3.5 s). After a listen he turns again only after hear_repeat_s (6 s), unless the sound comes from ≥ hear_side_deg (45°) to the left or right (2026-11-14). Again from the side → the source was behind: turns by the remainder (180° − reading); at most hear_turns (3). From the front → done. Silence → doubt, then searches (hear_seek, below)
    sound while roaming (he turns and drives almost all the time, no direction then) stops roaming, stands and listens; the next report gives the direction
    target appears while turning surprised, found sound; then talks back (below)
    come here: a person in view farther than come_stop_m + 0.3 m speaks (come_events = 1 report, a short call is enough) happy sound, drives toward the person and stops come_stop_m (0.4 m since 2026-11-22, was 0.8) short, following while they move; no planner path → straight toward her, at most 30 s; at most come_m per time (RiderPi −1 = no limit, simulator 0), again after come_repeat_s (8 s). Ends when the person is lost for 1 s (then the normal search) or the arc is blocked
    calling: voice keeps coming from the front (call_events = 3 reports), nobody in view searches there like "search": drives at most call_m (RiderPi −1 = no limit, then one 3 m leg per call; never more than seek_m; simulator 0) with the same conditions as every pet drive, then looks around (finds someone standing exactly behind). Again after call_repeat_s (45 s) at the earliest
    heard, then silence, nobody in view (hear_seek, 2026-11-19) direction known (turned to it, or voice from the front, one report is enough): searches there like calling. Unknown (stopped while roaming, then silence): looks around once, listening hear_listen_s per view; a report during a view → turns there, listens, drives there. Silence all round → search ends (sad). Lockout call_repeat_s
    driving in pet with the room map (search, calling, back to start) the planner gives the path (like home); the Bahnprüfung still brakes. Calling goal: if the room map shows a wall within 1 m ahead and more room behind, he searches behind (front and back sound the same); the goal ends before the wall. No path, or a detour over 1.5× the straight line → the other direction
    call without usable direction, room map with confirmed pose (call_openings 4, 2026-11-22) calls from another room arrive at ~0° whatever his heading (reverb, mics 4 mm apart). Instead of driving straight: next opening of the room map (border to unknown, frontier.next_goal in the planner thread, visited ones excluded, none with a path shorter than call_min_m 0.3 m; if he had turned toward the sound, openings off that direction cost call_dir_s_per_deg 0.1 s/° extra, 2026-11-22) → drive there with the path follower of home (tight spots, push, replanning; gives up only after two replans while stuck, time from the path length) → look around and listen (hear_listen_s standing per view, levels) → a person found there: drives to her like "come here" (no second call needed) → call_louder_db (3 dB) louder than so far = louder, either way on to the next opening; after call_openings openings or none left he stays and listens. Sound is not used while driving. Unit tests only
    search over, nobody found (seek_home, off since 2026-11-22; for a vanished person only with lost_home) drives back to the map start only with a start image (2026-11-22; the start is set on every stand-up and set-down, see mapping). The drive is home's (planner, pre-point, straight final run, measurement against the start image, pose correction). Without anchor or confirmed pose he stays where he is
    suddenly loud (hear_startle_db = 20 dB) after hear_quiet_s (8 s) of silence, nobody in view surprised, squeak, ducks 0.6 s, turns fast (turn_w). No retreat, no faint
    dozing, two reports in a row wakes: surprised, horch, detector back to active_hz
    lying after a faint air is not clear while someone speaks (last report < clear_s): eyes stay shut, no stand-up
    lying (set down from outside), two reports doubt, horch (direction is useless lying down)
    talking back: person in view (or just found by listening) speaks ≥ talk_min_events (2) reports, then pauses talk_pause_s (1.6 s) sound brabbeln, head tilt; at most every talk_repeat_s (7 s); with parrot_p (0.5) a learned voice sample instead, ≥ parrot_repeat_s (30 s) apart (audio.md)
    a voice sample gets confirmed (same phrase twice within 1 min while a person is in view, balancing or lying) or a confirmed one is recognized parrots it after parrot_delay_s (0.2 s), budgie-filtered, every time

    Pi cost: a few comparisons per control step; the sound direction itself as in audio.md.

    Faint¶

    vertigo, set down, eyes shut nap_s (10 s) → eyes open slowly over eyes_open_s (3 s) while looking left and right → anyone in view: eyes shut again, wait → nobody in view for clear_s (2.5 s): wake-up sound standup_warn_s (1.5 s) → stand up (253), cute + get-up sound → settle_s (6 s) without gestures. A fall (state 0) in pet shows vertigo; the scream comes from xgonav/emotion.py.

    It stands up by itself only if all hold:

    Condition Otherwise
    set down by itself (external set-down, fall, started lying) stays down
    still in mode pet (POST /stop, any mode change, web action 254) stand-up cancelled
    IMU within nap_tilt_deg (5°) and wheels within nap_moved_m (1 cm) of the resting pose; state unchanged and not 0/250/251/252; all three known stays down
    no detection in the image, any class among person/cat/dog, any confidence waits; gives up after nap_max_s (60 s)
    battery known and > battery_low as above
    fewer than faint_max (3) faints in 10 min scare instead of faint

    One attempt only.
